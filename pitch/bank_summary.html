Basis and data quality

  • GHG Protocol Corporate Standard, operational control, calendar year 2025 (first inventory = base year).
  • Scope 2 location-based; market-based is the same (15,993 t): no certificates or supplier factors, no residual mix for Kazakhstan.
  • Factors exactly as in the case file: IPCC 2006 Tier 1 fuels (CO2 only), refrigerant GWPs from IPCC AR5, Kazakhstan grid 0.8 kg/kWh. Refrigerant refills counted as leaks.
  • Not in the total (memo): Scope 3 Cat 12 packaging end of life about 3,214 t; CH4/N2O from fuel about 74 t; Cat 3 upstream fuel and grid losses not estimated (no factor in the case).
  • All 1,952 ledger lines traced to a rule, a factor and a reason (outputs/audit.csv); duplicates, taxes, advances, fines and per diem excluded with a reason; 58 lines carry a review note, none is unresolved.
